# IAM-journey

## Why IAM

I'm currently a computer science major that joined college with the intent of becoming a software engineer. However, with the rapid progression of ai, increase in layoffs, and overall lack of job security i've decided to switch my career goals towards becoming a IAM engineer. I will continue to pursue a major in computer science but all my extracurricular projects and study will be focused on IAM. IAM is perfect for me because it requires both technical skills and human judgement.
## What this repo is

A public portfolio documenting my 24-week structured path from 
foundations to job-ready IAM engineer. Every concept I study and 
every lab I complete gets documented here in my own words.

## Progress

**Current phase:** Phase 0 — Foundations (Week 1)

## Structure

- `concepts/` — written explanations of core IAM and security concepts
- `labs/` — hands-on lab notes and command outputs
- `artifacts/` — portfolio pieces like access control matrices
- `notes/` — news log and miscellaneous study notes
